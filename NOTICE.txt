Selected research-verification materials — version 1.0.0

This prepared archive has a deliberately limited scope. It contains selected
result-verification scripts, processed inputs, parameter descriptions and
reference values. It does not contain the core prediction software, training
implementation, trained weights or software-registration documents.

The verification scripts are organized from the reported calculations and
archived evaluation conventions. The necessary metric definitions overlap
with evaluation rules in the broader software; no claim of zero overlap or
new independent ownership is made.

No project-wide open-source license, transfer of ownership, or license to
excluded software assets is made by this notice. Any distribution and reuse
terms for this selected archive must be specified by the actual rights
holders. Contact the corresponding author identified in the accompanying
article for permission or licensing questions.

No third-party source libraries or model weights are redistributed here.
Python's standard library is sufficient for the verification scripts.
